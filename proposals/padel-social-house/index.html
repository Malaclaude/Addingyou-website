Measurement & ownership

We agree the baseline, off-peak windows and success measures before launch. Where the available data and audience size support it, we compare contacted customers with a holdout group to estimate incremental bookings. A booking match alone is not proof that a campaign caused it. We track opt-outs alongside results and validate visit completion before measuring return visits. Build & support is staff-led; Managed and Full Partnership include our ongoing campaign delivery. Documentation and training support staff ownership, with any later handover or reduced support arrangement agreed separately.

Scope & running costs

These are proposal options, subject to a final written scope and term. The £1,500 option assumes your team handles daily campaigns; the managed options include agreed campaign delivery. The £2,500 option includes an agreed refresh of the existing website and one month of social strategy, not ongoing social consultancy. The £3,500 option adds ongoing social consultancy, live padel trend tracking, detailed campaign content plans, staff guidance and agreed campaign content capture. Message usage, WhatsApp fees, phone rental and calls, advertising spend, bespoke AI builds and extra production are additional. Applicable VAT is additional.

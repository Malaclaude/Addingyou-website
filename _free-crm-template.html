Why do you need our Companies House number?

To confirm you're a real trading UK club before we create an account. It stops us handing out systems to people who aren't running a venue, which is what keeps this sustainable enough to give away.

We're not open yet. Can we still apply?

Yes, and honestly it's a good time to do it. Setting up how you'll capture and keep players before opening day is far easier than retrofitting it once you're busy. Just pick "we're not open yet" on the form.

How long until we get access?

Usually inside one working day. Every application is checked against Companies House and then read by one of us, rather than approved by a script, so it is not instant. If your club is a fit we email your login. If it isn't, we tell you rather than leaving you waiting.

What happens to our data?

It's your account and your data. We don't sell it, share it, or market to your players. You stay the data controller for your members and we act as processor, covered by a standard data processing agreement you'll get with your login. If you ever want out, you export your data and go.

Is there any support included?

Not on the free account, and we'd rather say so up front than pretend otherwise. You get the system and the documentation. If you want us in it with you, that's the paid build.

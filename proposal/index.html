Proposed attribution and commission rules

Baseline: record existing gym members and open gym enquiries before launch. Both are excluded from acquisition commission. We recognise that the gym is already growing through word of mouth, the website and your own social activity.

Qualifying journey: a new paying gym member joins through an ADDING YOU campaign link, or submits a tracked campaign enquiry and joins within 30 days. Email, WhatsApp and SMS links use channel-specific campaign tracking. Receiving or opening a message alone does not qualify.

Payment matching: retain the campaign source through checkout or match the enquiry to the paying membership using a member ID, email or phone number. Reception sign-ups qualify only where this evidence exists. Checkout tracking and access to payment records must be confirmed before launch; UTMs alone are not sufficient.

Commission proposal: 25% of qualifying gym subscription revenue actually collected, excluding VAT and refunds, for up to 12 months from each qualifying member’s first payment. No commission on unpaid, paused or cancelled periods. Report and reconcile monthly. This is recurring commission, not a fee on the first payment only.

Before agreement: confirm source precedence, duplicate handling and what happens to commission if the partnership ends. The attribution rules allocate commercial credit; they do not prove that every tracked member would otherwise have stayed away.

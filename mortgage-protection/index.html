Before we talk

Clear from the start.

Is the first month completely free?+

There is no AY management fee for the first 30 days. You pay your advertising budget directly through your ad account and cover agreed usage costs. We confirm those costs and the scope before starting.

What happens after 30 days?+

We review the campaign and lead quality together. If you choose to continue, the management fee is £1,500/month plus advertising and agreed usage costs. Paid management only starts by agreement.

Can you run Meta and Google Ads?+

Yes. We agree one starting channel for the pilot: Facebook and Instagram through Meta, or Google Search. The choice depends on your offer, audience and budget. Running both channels is separately scoped.

Can you use our existing database?+

Where appropriate, we can agree a reactivation journey for suitable past enquiries or clients. We first review the records, contact permissions and your firm’s requirements. The aim is to restart relevant conversations for your advisers, rather than send every contact the same message.

Do we have to replace our broker CRM?+

No. We provide an enquiry CRM, but review your current systems before recommending a setup. Any connection to your existing broker software, data transfer or replacement is agreed separately.

Does AI give mortgage or protection advice?+

No. Your advisers retain responsibility for advice and suitability. Any optional AI use, approved information, data access and handover to a person are agreed with your firm first.

Do you guarantee leads or clients?+

No. Results depend on your offer, market, budget and follow-up. We agree how to assess lead quality and progress with your team. Enquiries are opportunities, not completed sales.

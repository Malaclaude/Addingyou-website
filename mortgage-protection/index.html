A few things
worth knowing.

Does the AI replace our advisers?

No. It handles first contact, agreed questions, follow-up and booking. Your advisers provide the advice and build the relationship.

Can it answer questions and handle objections?

It uses information and responses agreed with your team. Questions about rates, product recommendations or suitability go to your advisers.

Can we use our existing leads and database?

Yes. We can start with your current lead providers, website enquiries and suitable existing contacts. We agree which records and contact permissions are appropriate before launch.

How does it improve over time?

We review conversations with your team, correct gaps and update the approved information. Changes are managed and checked.

Before we talk

Clear from the start.

Do we have to change our broker CRM?+

No. We supply a separate marketing CRM for enquiries, follow-up and bookings. Your existing broker system can stay in place for cases and advice. We check the available connections and agree any data transfer before building.

Can we give you the questions and answers?+

Yes. We work with your advisers to build the questions, business information and approved responses. We agree when the AI should book a call, stop following up or pass the conversation to a person.

Can the AI make calls and handle common objections?+

AI voice calls and messaging can form part of the agreed setup. The assistant can use your approved responses to common questions or concerns and help arrange an adviser conversation. Mortgage rates, product recommendations and suitability decisions stay with your advisers.

How does calendar booking work?+

We agree the adviser calendars, availability and appointment rules. The system offers suitable available times, records the booking and sends agreed reminders. We check compatibility with your calendar during setup.

Can it follow up our existing database?+

Yes, for suitable contacts your firm is permitted to approach. We agree separate follow-up for new enquiries, unfinished conversations and existing-customer reviews, including when each journey should stop.

Does the AI learn by itself?+

We manage improvements. We review conversations, correct gaps and update the approved information and follow-up with your team. The service does not rely on the AI changing its own advice or rules.

What does it cost?+

There is a paid setup stage and ongoing monthly management. We quote after reviewing your adviser numbers, volumes, AI channels and integrations. The scope, software and usage costs, and any applicable VAT are confirmed before you commit.

Can you also help us generate new leads?+

Yes. Meta or Google advertising can be scoped separately. This service can start with your existing lead providers, website enquiries and suitable customer records; buying more leads is not a requirement.

Do you guarantee appointments or revenue?+

No. Results depend on your enquiries, contact permissions, available appointments and how conversations progress. We agree the measures with you, such as response times, booked and attended calls, and completed cases.

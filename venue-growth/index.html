Before we talk.

Google Ads or Meta: which do I need?+

Google Search can reach people already looking for your activity. Meta can introduce your offer to people browsing Facebook and Instagram. We assess demand, budget and the offer before choosing a starting channel. Running both is separately scoped.

Do you guarantee leads or bookings?+

No. Results depend on your offer, market, budget and follow-up. We agree the campaign, deliverables and measurement with you. Enquiries are not the same as paid bookings.

Do I need to buy a CRM first?+

No. We provide your CRM and configure the enquiry pipeline and basic follow-up as part of the setup. If you already use a system, we’ll review it with you first. Any ongoing software or messaging charges are agreed upfront. Replacing your booking platform or building custom integrations is separate work.

What do you need from us?+

Access to your ad account, suitable photos or video, an agreed offer, advertising budget and a named person to follow up. You also need capacity to fulfil the bookings you’re seeking.

Do you work with other leisure venues?+

Yes, we can assess padel, fitness and other bookable or membership venues. Tell us what you operate and we’ll be direct about whether our experience and this package are a fit.

Who owns the campaign and customer data?+

Your advertising runs through your own ad account. You retain access to your customer records. The written scope sets out the accounts, access and handover arrangements before we begin.

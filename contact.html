Before you book

Quick answers.

How quickly can you get started?

Usually within a week of agreeing scope. We do a discovery call, agree what we are building, and start immediately. We do not have onboarding periods that last a month.

Is there a minimum contract length?

No. We work month to month. We prefer it that way because it means we have to keep earning the relationship. Most clients stay for a long time, but there is no clause forcing them to.

Do I need a big budget for Meta ads?

No. We have generated qualified leads on budgets under £500/month. The platform rewards well-built campaigns, not just big spend. We are happy to discuss what is realistic for your budget before we start.

Can I just get one thing done, not a full retainer?

Yes. CRM builds, AI assistants and one-off campaign setups are all available as project work. Tell us what you need and we will quote accordingly.
